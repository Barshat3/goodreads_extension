⚡ Get Your Free Serper API Key

Takes under 2 minutes — no credit card, no setup complexity.

  1. Go to serper.dev and click Get Started Free.
  2. Sign up with your Google account (one click — no form to fill out).
  3. You'll land on the dashboard. Your API Key is shown right on the page — click to copy it.
  4. Paste it into the field above and click Save Key. You're done!

📖 How to Use the Extension

Look up any book in seconds, right from the page you're reading.

  1. On any webpage, use your mouse to highlight a book title.
  2. Press the keyboard shortcut:
    Ctrl + B / ⌘ Cmd + B
  3. A floating card appears in the top-right corner with the Goodreads rating, vote count, and description.
  4. Click "View full book details" to open the full Goodreads page in a new tab.

Your API key is stored locally via chrome.storage.local and only sent to serper.dev — never anywhere else.
